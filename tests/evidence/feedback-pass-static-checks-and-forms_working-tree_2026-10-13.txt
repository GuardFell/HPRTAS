=========================================================================================

Version under test

The working tree on top of `aba6b29`, uncommitted at the time of the run: the feedback pass that
adds the record store, the input-validation policy, the appointment-confirmation outcomes, the
merging gateways and the end-to-end process. `git rev-parse --short HEAD` names the commit the

Date: 2026-10-13.  Tester: automated.

Why these are recorded separately
---------------------------------
`workers-and-models_working-tree_2026-10-13.txt` records what the workers and the models do when
they run. These checks are about the artefacts instead: that the models, the forms and the workers
agree with each other, that the diagrams follow the layout and BPMN rules, that the form validation
is the policy the repository documents, that every form still imports in the library Tasklist renders
it with, and that the exported diagrams are legible. Only the last needs anything outside Python and
Node.

The workers, the forms and the models agree
-------------------------------------------

    6 operational models, 43 forms, 8 workers

    PASS: the models, the forms and the workers agree

The check grew four rules with this pass, and each was made to fail before it was trusted: a call
activity has to name a process in `models/operational/`, what that process returns counts as
produced in the model that calls it, every user task has to carry a candidate group, and every
reference, NHS number and person's name has to carry the input-validation pattern. Removing one
candidate group from `core-5` and one pattern from `referral-check.form` reported exactly those two
problems; both were restored by re-running `apply_form_validation.py`.

The models were redrawn with `relayout_hprtas_bpmn.py`, which now prefers the layout that keeps the
lines off the activity boxes where one exists: it counts the flows that would cross a box under the
same rule the check applies, and picks the ordering that has none before it picks the one with the
fewest crossings. Two flows in `core-2` crossed a box at first; both are gone. One convergence in
`core-2` - the two payment-error repair paths into `N_F_CorrectPaymentRequest` - keeps its merge at
the task rather than in a gateway, because no ordering routed a boundary event's return across the
payment task's column without the line crossing it. That exception is recorded in the model itself,
in the task's documentation.

The forms are the validation policy, and they all still import
-------------------------------------------------------------
    $ python tools/apply_form_validation.py
    Every form already carries the validation policy.

    $ NODE_PATH=<scratch>/node_modules node tools/check_forms_formjs.mjs
    imported 43 of 43 forms
    treatment-booking: future-dated start date is enforced by the picker
    validation rules fire as intended
    PASS: the forms import and validate

`check_forms_formjs.mjs` is new: it imports every form into `@bpmn-io/form-js-viewer` - the library
Tasklist renders forms with - and then runs each field through the same validator the renderer uses.
It asserts that a complete `appointment-confirmation` submission reports nothing, that a reference in
the wrong shape is refused with the message the form carries, that a name of digits alone is refused,
and that an NHS number that is not ten digits is refused. It needs `jsdom` and
`@bpmn-io/form-js-viewer` installed where Node can find them; it is not part of the Python checks
because it is the only one that needs packages.

    VERIFICATION PASSED

The exports are better read back than they were: 82-96% of the words come back, against 69-82% for
the seven models before this pass. Three drawing faults were found and fixed while making that true,
the first two in `bpmn_render.py` and the third in the exporter, which has its own drawing switch:

- a boundary event was drawn as a rounded box rather than as the double ring the notation gives it;
- a call activity was drawn as an ordinary task with no way to tell it invokes another process;
- the exporter drew **nothing at all** for the kinds it did not know, so once the two glyphs above
  existed the boundary events and the call activities disappeared from the exports - silently, since
  the OCR check measures words and the words were still drawn. It now refuses to draw an unknown
  kind rather than dropping it.

The layout check also grew a rule while this was being looked at, because a picture caught what the
numbers had not: a flow's label must not be set across an activity or a gateway. The first run of it
failed on 20 labels across the six operational models - branch conditions written over the diamond
of the gateway they belong to, so the glyph and the words obscured each other. The placer that
chooses label positions now avoids the shapes (and has more positions to choose from), the diagrams
were redrawn, and the rule passes on all eight models. `relayout_hprtas_bpmn.py` had the same
information missing: it now prefers the ordering whose lines keep off the activity boxes, and its
flow-label placer knows where the activities and gateways are.

What these checks do not cover
------------------------------
- **No signed-in user and no enforced role.** Every user task carries a candidate group and the
  bindings check requires it, but nothing authenticates a user or stops one claiming another role's
  task on this installation; that is `DEF-07`.
- **The forms are exercised through the library, not through Tasklist.** The validation rules fire in
  the renderer's own validator; a person filling a form in through Tasklist is still not what was
  run, which is `DEF-08`.
- **The export check measures word coverage, not layout.** A diagram can read 90% and still be ugly;
  the layout check is what measures overlap and containment, and it passes separately.
