SCENARIO
--------
The provider takes the payment but the confirmation does not come back because
of a communication failure. The transaction must be marked for investigation and
**no** automatic re-request may be issued.

DATA AND ACTIONS
----------------
  chargeAmount     = 250
  fundingRoute     = "patient"
  paymentReference = "PAY-2026-0001"
  paymentOutcome   = "success_no_confirmation"

ACTUAL RESULT
-------------
PASS.

  ok 18 - payment-processing: a payment taken without a confirmation is marked for
          investigation (TC-09, BR-07, AC-04)

  paymentStatus         = "completed"    (the money was taken)
  paidAmount            = 250
  confirmationReceived  = false
  requiresInvestigation = true
  transactionReference  = returned and retained

The distinction the case cares about is preserved: the payment is recorded as
taken, but flagged for investigation rather than treated as settled. The
transaction is held in the provider ledger against its payment reference, so if
the process were to attempt the payment again the worker would return that same
transaction as a duplicate and take no further money (tests 19 and 20 cover the
duplicate and retry behaviour).

NOT COVERED HERE
----------------
TC-09 expects that no automatic re-request is issued. That is a decision taken by
the model, by routing the flagged transaction to investigation instead of back to
the payment activity, so it is a property of the operational model rather than of
this worker. What is evidenced here is the flag the model needs in order to make
that decision, and the ledger behaviour that stops a re-request from charging
again if one were made.
