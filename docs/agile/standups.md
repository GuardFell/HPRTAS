# Standups

> **Agile Workshop, Part 7.** A standup is short: progress and blockers, not a technical
> discussion. Every update must be supported by evidence - "I worked on the model" is not an
> update; "TB-008 drafted and deployed, deployment log at `tests/evidence/...`" is.

## What each student covers

1. What I completed
2. What I will do next
3. What is blocking me
4. What evidence supports my update
5. Whether I need second-owner support
6. Whether any handover is required
