# Sprint Reviews

> **Agile Workshop, Part 8.** At each review the group demonstrates the **current integrated
> increment** against the sprint goal, distinguishes finished from unfinished work, explains
> decisions and limitations, gathers feedback and updates the backlog. Every member must be able
> to explain their own contribution.
