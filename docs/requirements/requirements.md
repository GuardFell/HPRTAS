# Requirements

> Every requirement is traceable to a source: a pathway stage, a stated business rule (BR-###) or
> a documented assumption (AS-###). IDs stay stable once assigned.
>
> Convention: `FR-###` functional, `NFR-###` non-functional, `BR-###` business rule
> (`docs/case-study-summary.md` section 5), `AS-###` assumption (section 10).
