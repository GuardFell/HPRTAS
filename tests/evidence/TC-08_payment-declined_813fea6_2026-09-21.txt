SCENARIO
--------
The simulated provider declines the payment. The patient and the responsible
administrative team must be told, the appointment must not be confirmed, and a
retry must be possible without a duplicate booking or a second charge.

DATA AND ACTIONS
----------------
  chargeAmount     = 250
  fundingRoute     = "patient"
  paymentReference = "PAY-2026-0001"
  paymentOutcome   = "declined"

Then the same payment reference is retried once the payment can succeed:

  attempt 1  paymentOutcome = "declined"
  attempt 2  paymentOutcome = "completed"

ACTUAL RESULT
-------------
PASS.

  ok 17 - payment-processing: a declined payment is reported so the patient and
          team can be notified (TC-08, EX-08)
  ok 20 - payment-processing: a declined attempt may be retried without a double
          charge (EX-08)

  Attempt 1  paymentStatus = "declined"
             paidAmount    = null
             requiresInvestigation = false
  Attempt 2  paymentStatus = "completed"
             transactionReference different from the declined attempt's reference

The declined attempt is returned with its status and the reference of the
attempt, so the process can notify the patient and the administrative team. No
amount is recorded, and because nothing was taken the reference is not held in
the provider ledger, so attempt 2 is allowed to proceed through to completion
rather than being reported as a duplicate. A patient who was declined therefore
is not blocked, and is not charged twice.

NOT COVERED HERE
----------------
TC-08 expects "no duplicate booking". The booking is created by the treatment
worker, not here; the duplicate check for it is in the TC-12 evidence item. The
notification itself is a user task or message in the operational model, which
does not exist yet, so only the data the notification would need is evidenced
here.
