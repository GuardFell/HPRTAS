SCENARIO
--------
The external treatment, laboratory and imaging service is temporarily
unavailable while a treatment booking is being made. The booking must stay
pending, further attempts must be recorded, and no duplicate appointment may be
created.

DATA AND ACTIONS
----------------
An authorised Treatment Booking Request is sent to the worker twice with the
simulated service unavailable, then again once it recovers:

  attempt 1  treatmentOutcome = "unavailable"
  attempt 2  treatmentOutcome = "unavailable"
  attempt 3  treatmentOutcome = "available"

The three attempts share one process instance, so they resolve to the same
booking key.

ACTUAL RESULT
-------------
PASS.

  ok 14 - treatment-availability: an unavailable service leaves the booking pending
          and records the attempt (TC-12, EX-07)
  ok 15 - treatment-availability: retrying after an outage creates no second
          appointment (AC-10)

  attempt 1 and 2  externalServiceAvailable = false
                   bookingStatus            = "pending"
                   appointmentReference     = null
                   treatmentRetryCount      = 1, then 2
  attempt 3        bookingStatus            = "confirmed"
                   appointmentReference     = "TRT-2251799813685249:treatment-appointment"
                                              (TRT- followed by the booking key, which is the
                                               process instance key and the activity)
                   duplicateAttempt         = true

Attempts 1 and 2 leave the booking pending and increment treatmentRetryCount, so
the retry is recorded without an appointment existing. Attempt 3 creates the
appointment; a further attempt returns that same reference with
duplicateAttempt = true rather than creating another one, which is the "no
duplicate appointments" requirement.

NOT COVERED HERE
----------------
TC-12 also expects "the responsible team is notified". That is a user task or a
message in the operational model, not worker behaviour, so it is not evidenced
here. The service was made unavailable through the scenario variable and the
configuration switch rather than by stopping a real service, because the service
is simulated (AS-03).
