# Requirements

> Every requirement is traceable to a source: a pathway stage, a stated business rule (BR-###) or
> a documented assumption (AS-###). IDs stay stable once assigned.
>
> Convention: `FR-###` functional, `NFR-###` non-functional, `BR-###` business rule
> (`docs/case-study-summary.md` section 5), `AS-###` assumption (section 10).
>
> **Where implemented / Where tested** name the artefact that carries the requirement at the time of
> writing. Element references are BPMN ids inside the named model. `core-1` to `core-4` are the four
> executable models in `models/operational/`, and `strategic` is
> `models/strategic/patient-pathway-all-entities.bpmn` (non-executable, the view of all entities).
> The models carry these IDs in their own element documentation, so every reference below can be
> checked inside the model.
>
> ### Status of the traceability update (21 Sep 2026)
>
> The first draft of this list referenced the three models of the first operational edition
> (`referral-to-appointment`, `treatment-authorisation-and-booking`,
> `clinic-letter-and-pathway-monitoring`). Those models were replaced by the four `core-N` models
> (see the commit that migrated to them), and the replacement was not a rename: elements were
> split, merged and re-identified, so every reference here was re-pointed by hand against the
> element documentation of the current models rather than by text substitution.
>
