# Contribution Matrix

> Record contribution with evidence as the project runs - it cannot be reconstructed at the end.
> Evidence must be a repository path, a commit or an artefact reference.
>
> **How this table is filled in.** Each member records their own contribution, with the group
> agreeing the share at the sprint review. M5 has recorded the M5 rows, and M4 recorded the M4 row
> in Sprint 4. **The M1-M3 rows were compiled on 19 October 2026 from the first- and second-owner
> assignments recorded in `../backlog/sprint-backlogs.md` and `../backlog/product-backlog.md` and
> from the artefacts those rows name**, so that the record is complete for the submission; each of
> M1-M3 should read their own row and correct anything it understates. Note that a member's
> obligation on a shared item is not limited to the row where they are the first owner: where a task
> has a second owner, that member's work on it belongs in their row too.
